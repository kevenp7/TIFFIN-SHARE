About TIFFIN SHARE

TIFFIN SHARE is a food-tech startup redefining how communities discover and enjoy homemade meals. We create a trusted digital marketplace where home cooks, local chefs, and everyday food lovers connect in one seamless experience.

Our mission is simple: give passionate food creators a platform to grow their home-based kitchen businesses while helping customers access fresh, affordable, authentic meals from nearby kitchens they can trust.

How It Works

  • Home chefs showcase their meals: Independent cooks list dishes prepared with care and authenticity.
  • Customers choose by location: Users browse local kitchens and discover meals available in their area.
  • Orders are placed in minutes: A simple ordering flow makes it easy to select favorites and complete checkout.
  • Fresh food reaches the doorstep: Meals are delivered with convenience, trust, and community-first service.

Our Mission

To make homemade food more accessible, more scalable, and more trusted — while helping small home kitchens build sustainable businesses and reach a broader audience.

Our Vision

To build a thriving food community where every neighborhood has access to delicious home-cooked meals and every home cook has the tools to grow with confidence.
